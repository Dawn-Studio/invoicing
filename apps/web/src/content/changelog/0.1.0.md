### Invoices

- Drafts save as you type, with a live preview of the printed page beside the form.
- Issue invoices with your own numbering pattern; issued invoices are locked and kept exactly as sent.
- Export PDFs, print, or open a ready-written email in your mail app.
- Record full and partial payments; overdue invoices stand out.

### Clients, items and taxes

- Save clients once and their details, currency and terms fill in on every invoice.
- Saved items for the work you bill often, tax rates per line, discounts.
- Any currency, with amounts never mixed between currencies.

### Reports

- Dashboard with what's owed, what's late and what came in each month.
- Revenue by client and a tax summary for any period, with CSV and PDF exports for your accountant.

### Your data

- Everything is stored on your computer: no account, no cloud.
- Separate workspaces for separate businesses.
- Automatic daily backups, plus backup files you can save anywhere and restore.

### The app

- Command palette (⌘K / Ctrl+K), keyboard shortcuts everywhere, native menus.
- Light and dark, ten colour palettes.
- macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux; updates install themselves on Windows and Linux.
